# ReadyVibe-Skills v1.1.0

Project-aware legal drafting workflows now accompany the existing launch and compliance skills. The templates help an agent inspect a product, identify which policy pages apply, prepare a project-specific draft, and leave unresolved facts or legal judgments for the owner and qualified counsel.

## Highlights

- Terms of Service, Privacy Policy, Cookie Policy, and Returns/Refunds templates are available to the relevant standalone legal, commerce, and launch skills.
- Skills select templates from observed product behavior, data flows, storage, providers, checkout, and account controls. They do not force every site to publish every policy or add a cookie banner without evidence.
- The workflow separates observed project facts from owner decisions, keeps unresolved markers out of publishable drafts, follows the project's existing design system when implementing pages, and verifies resulting behavior.
- The unified marketplace bundle preserves intentional drafting placeholders and policy links inside legal template references while rejecting unresolved placeholders elsewhere.
- Synthetic scenarios cover policy selection, conditional clauses, unresolved markers, installed references, and companion fallback behavior.

## Install

```bash
npx skills add moh-obaida/ReadyVibe-Skills#v1.1.0 --skill launch-all
```

Install the complete collection with:

```bash
npx skills add moh-obaida/ReadyVibe-Skills#v1.1.0 --all
```

The release also includes the unified marketplace ZIP for standalone marketplace installation.

## Scope

These templates are practical drafting references, not legal advice or a guarantee of compliance. Applicability and legal judgments remain subject to project-owner and jurisdiction-specific review. ReadyVibe does not invent project facts or claim that a site is legally compliant.
